Purpose of the app

The app provides tools to calculate aluminium fabrication measurements, organize customer and work records, and prepare billing summaries and report images. It is a calculation aid and does not replace professional judgment, on-site measurement, product specifications, or applicable building and safety requirements.

02

Calculations and your responsibility

You are responsible for checking measurements, units, inputs, selected rates, and results before ordering materials, cutting components, installing products, or making business decisions. The billing breakdown calculates the window work-area amount from square feet excluding fanlight area, and calculates the fanlight amount from fanlight quantity and its rate. Review those inputs and the final bill before relying on a report.

Results may be affected by incorrect inputs, settings, rounding rules, or software defects. Verify critical dimensions and amounts independently.
