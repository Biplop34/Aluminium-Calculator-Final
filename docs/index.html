Measurement & fabrication companion

Plan aluminum work with clarity.

Aluminum Calculator is an Android application designed to help users calculate aluminum frame, shutter, and glass dimensions, organize customer jobs, and back up app data to their own Google Drive account when they choose.

Android application · Measurement tools · Optional Google Drive backup

Dimension calculations

Calculate dimensions for aluminum frame sections, shutter sections, and glass using measurements entered by the user.

Customer job records

Keep customer details, measurements, and calculation results organized in saved jobs on the device.

Measurement utilities

Use measurement and unit-conversion tools to support common fabrication calculations.

Google Drive backup

When configured, back up and restore app data using the Google account selected and authorized by the user.

Your data, your choice

Job details and calculation history are stored locally on the device. If Google Drive backup is enabled, the app can transfer its backup to the private app data area of the Google account you authorize. Backup is optional; local app features can be used without connecting an account.
