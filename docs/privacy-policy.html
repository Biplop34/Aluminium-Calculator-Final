Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aluminum Calculator Android application (“the app”) handles information when you use it. The app helps users calculate aluminum fabrication measurements and manage related job records.

In brief: Information entered for calculations and saved jobs is stored locally on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup, the app uses the Google account you select and authorize to store and restore a backup in that account's private app data area.

1. Information you may enter

Depending on the features you use, information may include customer name, phone number and location; aluminum and glass selections; measurements; calculation results; saved job records; and measurement-conversion history. Enter only information you are authorized to store.

2. Local storage

The app stores working data on your Android device using local app preferences and storage. This can include saved jobs, customer details associated with jobs, calculation settings, and conversion history. This information remains on the device unless you delete records, clear app data, uninstall the app, or use a backup/export feature.

3. Google Drive backup and restore

Google Drive backup is optional. If you connect a Google account and grant the requested Google Drive app-data permission, the app can create or update a backup file in that account's private appDataFolder. The app uses this area to save and retrieve its backup; it is not a general public folder in your Drive.

The backup may contain app data described above, including customer information and measurements you have saved. The app uses the authorized account to upload, find, and download the backup for restore. Google processes account authentication and Drive storage under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

You control whether to connect an account and use backup/restore. You can revoke the app's Google access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized Drive operations but does not necessarily delete a backup already stored in Drive. Manage or remove that backup through the relevant Google account/app controls where available.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide calculation features, display and manage saved job records, preserve relevant app history, and perform backup or restore when you request or enable those features.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The described backup function sends backup data to Google Drive in the Google account you authorize. The app does not automatically make customer details publicly visible. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or Google account may be able to access information available through those accounts. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete the relevant records or remove the app's local data. A Drive backup may remain in your Google account until it is deleted there. Deleting a local job does not necessarily remove an earlier backup copy, and deleting a Drive backup does not automatically clear local app data.

7. Security

The app relies on Android application storage protections and Google's account authorization and Drive security features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and Google account.

8. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose fabrication calculator and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the project maintainer using the support link below.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub project support page.
